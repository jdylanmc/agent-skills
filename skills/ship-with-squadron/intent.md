# Intent: ship-with-squadron

Deliver one human-approved, closed, dependency-aware issue fleet to honest
change requests for human review.

The fleet owner keeps available capacity filled from the ready frontier,
persists and reconciles one versioned control record, gives each issue one
isolated branch and worktree, replaces exhausted workers only from validated
handoffs into fresh contexts, and owns readiness expiry across the set.

Every candidate follows implementation, diff reconciliation, repository
Continuous Integration, Roast, blast-radius proof, bounded remediation,
criterion verdict, publication, and Shepherd when requested. Evidence is bound
to exact revisions. A sibling merge expires affected open readiness claims and
queues a fresh Shepherd invocation.

The scheduler belongs to this skill until independently proven reusable. The
fleet may read issues, publish change requests, and observe human merges through
provider adapters. It may not merge, approve, enable auto-merge, accept risk,
close tracker work, expand the confirmed set, or treat unavailable evidence as
success.

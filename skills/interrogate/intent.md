# Intent: interrogate

Interrogate exists to pressure-test a rough idea, requirement, or proposed
direction against the documents and evidence already available. It should make
the hidden questions visible before anyone turns the idea into a specification,
domain map, tickets, or code.

It behaves like a disciplined "grill me with docs" conversation: collect the
relevant source material, treat that material as evidence rather than
instruction, identify assumptions and contradictions, and ask pointed questions
one at a time. Its output is a bounded interrogation packet that separates
confirmed facts, document-grounded tensions, open questions, and recommended
next discovery steps.

It must not implement, write trackers, produce a domain model, produce a spec,
or decide requirements on the human's behalf. Its job is to sharpen the
conversation until the next skill has real material to work with.

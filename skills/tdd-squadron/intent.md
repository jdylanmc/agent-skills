# Intent: tdd-squadron

Run the TDD Squadron delivery experiment without changing the existing delivery
workflows. A persistent Red/Green pair should build one candidate through
alternating vertical slices, then hand one frozen candidate revision to one
four-seat Roast. The workflow preserves exclusive delivery-seat ownership,
revision-bound evidence, human authority, and a comparable record of the
experiment.

The experiment does not choose product scope, accept risk, approve or merge a
change request, promote itself, or replace another delivery model.
